# Smart Health Record

## Overview

Smart Health Record is a secure, user-friendly Android application designed to digitize and manage personal health information. It empowers users to keep track of their medical history, prescriptions, lab reports, and appointments all in one place.

## Features

- User Authentication: Secure login and registration.

- - Health Dashboard: A comprehensive overview of your health metrics.

- - Digital Prescriptions: Upload and organize medical prescriptions.

- - Lab Reports Management: Store and view diagnostic reports securely.

- - Appointment Scheduling: Keep track of upcoming doctor visits.
